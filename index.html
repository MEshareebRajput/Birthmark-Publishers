What is hybrid publishing?

Hybrid publishing is the fairest, most lucrative, and most ethical option for authors. You get a full professional publishing team — without giving away your rights or your profits.

Why hybrid

Three ways to publish. Only one puts authors first.

Traditional publishing takes authors' rights and profits. Self-publishing offers zero professional support. Hybrid publishing gives you both — support and ownership.

Option 01

Traditional Publishing

A professional team, but the publisher takes your rights and the majority of your profits.

  • Keep your rights
  • Keep your profits
  • Professional support
Option 02

Self-Publishing

You keep ownership, but you're on your own — with zero professional support.

  • Keep your rights
  • Keep your profits

Birthmark Option 03

Hybrid Publishing

A full publishing team and a marketing & publicity firm — while the rights and profits stay in your hands.

  • Keep your rights
  • Keep your profits
  • Professional support
  • Marketing & publicity included

What makes us different

Other hybrid publishers stop at publishing. We market and publicize your book.

Marketing and publicity are typically a responsibility put onto the author. At Birthmark Publishers, they're part of the partnership — we manage every aspect of the publishing process so your book reaches its readers.

FAQ

Everything you need to know about hybrid publishing with Birthmark. Still curious? We'd love to hear from you.

Ask Us Anything
What is hybrid publishing?
Hybrid publishing gives authors the professional support of a publishing house while they keep their rights and profits — the fairest, most lucrative and ethical option for authors.
How is Birthmark different from other hybrid publishers?
We're both a hybrid publisher and a marketing and publicity firm. Instead of putting marketing and publicity on the author, we handle it as part of the partnership.
Do I keep the rights to my book?
Yes. Birthmark was founded so authors no longer lose their rights and profits. We put profits back in the hands of authors.
What genres do you publish?
All fiction and non-fiction genres, for first-time and previously published authors alike.
When will your first books be available?
Our first batch of books will be available in 2027. Keep an eye on our Spotlight Series.
